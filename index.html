What is this?

A decision-support simulation comparing three strategies for rolling out an internal AI tool in a banking organisation. 100 synthetic employees interact through a social network, and their opinions evolve over 50 time steps based on peer influence, management pressure, and training.

How does it work?

The model uses Modified Friedkin-Johnsen opinion dynamics. Each agent has an innate opinion (anchoring point) and a susceptibility to external influence. Three channels affect opinions: peer influence through the social network, a decaying management signal, and ramping training effects. Champions amplify peer influence by 2.5x. Training permanently shifts innate opinions upward (accumulated learning). Change fatigue dampens management and training effects.

Data sources

The synthetic workforce is calibrated on real German labor market data from Bundesagentur für Arbeit (September 2025, Insurance & Finance sector). Demographics, roles, and departments reflect actual workforce composition patterns.

Disclaimer

This is an illustrative simulation for decision support, not a prediction of actual outcomes. Synthetic data only — not representative of any real organisation's workforce.
